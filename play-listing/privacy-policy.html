Word Search - Puzzle Games Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the Android app Word Search - Puzzle Games (com.drygonkiller.wordsearch).

Game data on your device

The game works offline. The app stores your preferences, saved puzzles, completed Daily Challenges, hints, coins and streaks locally on your device. The app does not require an account. We do not operate a game server or upload your puzzle progress.

Your game data stays on your device until you clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. We do not retain a server copy of your progress.

Advertising and consent

The app uses Google AdMob to serve ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage advertising consent when required. These services use an internet connection. Google may receive and share information such as your IP address, Advertising ID, other device identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention.

Google processes and retains this information under its own policies. See Google’s privacy policy, how Google uses data from apps that use its services, and how Google retains data.

Your choices

When regional privacy options are available, you can revisit your choices in the app under Settings → Ad privacy options. You can also manage your Advertising ID through your device's Android settings.
